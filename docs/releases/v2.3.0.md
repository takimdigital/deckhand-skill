# Deckhand 2.3.0 — more designs, compared, checked before you see them

**Browse more.** A pick shows 4 to 12 designs. "More · N left" brings the next ones, up to 30 per try. The designs
already on screen keep their places, and nothing is shown twice.

**Compare before and after.** Drag a line across the section to see your original on one side and the new design
on the other. It is on by default; press C to turn it off.

**Report what breaks.** Flag a design that does not work and say why. "Create the error report" writes one file
with everything needed to reproduce each problem. It stays on your machine.

**Every design is checked before you see it.** Deckhand now tries every design in its catalog against a real
section of its kind, the way a try would. A design that cannot work is never offered, and one that would lose
most of your content comes last. The check found and fixed several problems:
- Base UI designs lost every button. They now carry your buttons, like their Radix versions.
- A footer or navbar showing your plan's menu keeps your links.
- shadcn login and signup blocks failed to load. They now work, and they take your own form.
- A design's "© 2026 Acme" line becomes yours.

**1,247 designs, up from 773.** Two new MIT libraries: Shadcn UI Blocks (418 heroes, pricing, FAQs, testimonials,
teams, footers, navbars, logins…) and blocks.so (55 logins, stats, dialogs, tables). shadcn/studio was refused:
its licence adds a Commons Clause, which is not MIT.

**Each project keeps its own settings and keys.** Before, a domain, provider or key saved in one project showed up
in every other one. Now they stay in the project, and Deckhand asks you once where they should live
(`dh profile where`). Your own details, such as your name, stay shared, so no project asks them again.

**Copy that sounds like a machine is caught.** `dh slop check` flags AI-sounding copy (clichés, "It's not X, it's
Y", em dashes everywhere, lists of three) in 11 languages, with what to write instead. The brand step fails on
slop copy. A real customer quote can be marked `dh:slop-ok` and stays as written. The language files are first
drafts: if a good line is flagged, mark it and tell us.
